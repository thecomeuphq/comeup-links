Who it's for

Questions

Is anything free moving behind a paywall?

No. The free tools stay free.

What do I get?

A spreadsheet (.xlsx) with 10 tabs and a printable PDF of 10 pages (one per tab). After checkout, you land on a page with both downloads.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to your Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. It's easiest to fill in on a computer or tablet. The PDF prints on regular letter paper.

Does it figure my taxes?

No. The Shift Pay Planner shows gross pay before taxes. For take-home, use your paystub. The free OT Tax Spike Safeguard gives you an estimate of what withholding may take from overtime.

What if I'm not on a 2-2-3 or 4-on-4-off?

Enter the number of shifts you work each week to adapt the planner to your rotation.
