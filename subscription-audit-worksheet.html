Quick answers

How often should I run this audit?

Monthly is the habit that works — tie it to a payday so it takes five minutes while you're already looking at your money. At minimum, run it every 3 months: that's how long it takes a forgotten $12.99 charge to quietly become $39 gone. Any time you sign up for anything new, add it to the list the same day.

What's the free-trial trap?

A "free" trial converts to a paid plan automatically unless you cancel before the deadline — and companies count on you forgetting. The defense is mechanical: the day you start any trial, set a phone reminder for 2–3 days before it ends. If you can't remember the last time you opened the app, cancel now and re-subscribe later if you miss it. You almost never do.

Is annual billing actually cheaper than monthly?

Usually — annual plans commonly run 15–25% less than paying monthly (e.g. $119/year vs. $14.99/month = $179.88/year, a $60 difference). But the discount only wins if you'll use the service all year and you've already kept it through a full audit. Rule: monthly while you're testing, annual only for proven keeps. Paying a year upfront for something you kill in month three is the most expensive option of all.
