About

Built after shift.

The Come Up HQ is a one-man build. I work a full-time job, then I clock in on my own dream: free money tools and straight numbers for people working paycheck to paycheck.

No investors. No handouts. No guru courses. I started building in public in October 2026, and the rule has not changed: real numbers or it doesn't go up.

On one side of the shelf: the paycheck calculator, the debt calculator, and the free reset sheet. On the other side: BG Collections, everyday picks from stores I link to. Some links may earn us a commission at no cost to you.

Everything here is educational information, not financial advice. I am showing my work as I go — not pretending I already made it.
