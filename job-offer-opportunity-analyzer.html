Straight answers

What counts as a real raise?

Not just hourly pay. A raise is only a raise if it beats taxes, commute costs, childcare, uniforms, training, and the weekly strain on your life. This calculator makes you compare the real take-home after those costs.

How is the tax estimate calculated?

It uses a 2026 single-filer estimate: 7.65% FICA, the 2026 standard deduction of $16,100, and a default 4% state estimate. It gives you a practical take-home check, not a legal tax return.

Should I care about a bonus if it’s not guaranteed?

Only if it is reliable. This tool lets you plug in the annual bonus value, but your likely answer is: a guaranteed raise beats a possible bonus every time.
