What to watch before saying yes to more hours

Taxes are not flat. Extra overtime raises your gross pay, but your tax burden also climbs. The real win is the take-home after FICA, federal tax, and state tax.
Night and weekend premiums matter. A $2 premium late at night or on the weekend changes your effective pay rate, and some workers forget to count the difference.
Time is part of the equation. A good shift is not always one with the highest sticker rate. Sometimes a lower hourly rate with predictable hours keeps the paycheck steadier.
