What this wage actually looks like

A common middle-ground wage for warehouse, service, and production work. Extra OT hours matter fast here.

  • Gross is not take-home. Federal and state withholding, FICA, and deductions come out before you see the money. The overtime calculator shows the split.
  • 40 hours is an assumption. Shift work rarely delivers exactly 40. Run your real hours and any differentials through the shift calculator for a truer number.
  • One missed shift is $160.00. That is what an hours cut, a sick day, or a schedule change actually costs you at this rate.

Worth knowing

At $20 an hour, a single missed shift is $160. That is the number to think about when a schedule changes or hours get cut — it is a quarter of a full week, gone in one day.
