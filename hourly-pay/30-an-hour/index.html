What this wage actually looks like

Strong income for a single worker, but higher taxes and less flexibility if overtime becomes the norm.

  • Gross is not take-home. Federal and state withholding, FICA, and deductions come out before you see the money. The overtime calculator shows the split.
  • 40 hours is an assumption. Shift work rarely delivers exactly 40. Run your real hours and any differentials through the shift calculator for a truer number.
  • One missed shift is $240.00. That is what an hours cut, a sick day, or a schedule change actually costs you at this rate.

Worth knowing

At $30 an hour, overtime is often taxed at a higher marginal rate. Check the take-home on the extra hours, not the sticker rate — the OT Tax Spike Safeguard shows the split.
