How to start

  1. Open the spreadsheet and read the Start Here tab first: simple instructions, a color guide, and important information before you begin.
  2. Fill in the yellow cells with your own numbers. The green cells are formulas, so leave those alone.
  3. Numbers labeled Example show how a filled-in sheet looks. Swap them for yours.
  4. In the Shift Pay Planner, enter the number of shifts you work each week to adapt the planner to your rotation.
  5. Then open First 4 Paydays: a step-by-step plan for your next four checks.

It's easiest to fill in on a computer or tablet. The PDF prints on regular letter paper.
