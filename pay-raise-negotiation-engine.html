Straight answers

How should I choose my target raise?

Base it on added responsibilities, measurable results, reliable attendance, skills, and the value of the work—not just a number pulled from thin air. Check current local wage ranges where possible, and treat the target as a starting point for a conversation, not a promise.

Is overtime really equal to a raise?

No. Overtime depends on available hours and costs extra time away from home. This comparison only shows the gross hours needed to earn the same added gross dollars; it does not claim the two options are equal in stability, benefits, or quality of life.

Why is the take-home amount only an estimate?

Your actual paycheck depends on federal and state taxes, benefits, deductions, filing status, and withholding elections. The percentage here is a simple planning assumption, not a tax calculation.

What if my employer says no?

Ask what specific results or skills would support a review, agree on measurable checkpoints, and set a date to revisit the conversation. Don’t accept a vague promise as guaranteed future pay.
