Straight answers

Do I have to do them in order?

Mostly, yes — the weeks build on each other. Week 1 finds the leaks, Week 2 plugs them, Week 3 attacks debt with the money you freed up, and Week 4 puts your overtime to work. Inside a single week, though, you can swap days around to fit your shift schedule. Just don't skip Day 1 or Day 30: those two scores are how you prove the reset worked.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. There's no restart and no penalty — pick up the next day with the move you missed, or jump to today's and catch the missed one on your next day off. Your check-offs stay saved in this browser, and your streak counts consecutive days completed in order, so a gap just pauses the run, it doesn't erase your progress.

Is it really only 3 minutes?

Yes. Every move is one small, specific action inside a free tool — add up a number, get a score, set one dollar amount. No spreadsheets to build, no accounts to connect. The optional weekly planner is a separate purchase; you can complete the full reset without it.

Does it cost anything?

The reset and all 30 daily tools are free. The optional Paycheck-to-Paycheck Weekly Planner is $6.95; buying it is not required to complete the reset.
