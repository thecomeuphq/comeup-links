04 / Three repeatable plays

Copy the mechanics, not the product.

Each route begins with a channel-native promise, uses existing model APIs, and gets paid before technical complexity becomes the project.

01

Consumer demo loop

PAINFUL MOMENT → 15-SECOND DEMO → MICRO-CREATOR → LOW-FRICTION SUBSCRIPTION
  1. Pick a result that is visually obvious: reply, score, scan, or transformation.
  2. Build a mobile shell, API call, and paywall in 2–4 weeks; no custom model.
  3. Seed 5–20 small niche creators with a flat fee or revenue share before paid ads.
  4. Track revenue per 1,000 views by creator; scale only what converts.
  5. Optimize the first “magic moment” and paywall before adding reach.
02

B2B wow-demo

EXPENSIVE WORKFLOW → 60-SECOND PROOF → BUILD IN PUBLIC → FREEMIUM SAAS
  1. Choose a business pain where one demo makes the time saving undeniable.
  2. Build in 2–3 weeks on managed infrastructure; sell access to model output.
  3. Post demos, screenshots, failures, and revenue progress on X or LinkedIn.
  4. Start freemium or usage-based; add enterprise tiers only when customers pull for them.
  5. Automate repetitive operations before adding headcount.
03

Paid transformation

$100–500 SERVICE → INSTANT AI OUTPUT → ONE-TIME PACK → SHAREABLE RESULT + SEO
  1. Target an output people already pay for: headshots, résumés, designs, or mockups.
  2. Build upload → model API → gallery → download in 1–2 weeks.
  3. Use one-time $29–$59 packages or credits to match the tangible output.
  4. Launch with before/after proof; the transformation becomes the ad.
  5. Publish durable “[thing] AI generator” search pages while social spikes demand.
05 / The verdict

The leanest first bet is a demoable consumer utility.

Not because consumer apps are easy—they are not—but because this route has the clearest low-capital proof: a narrow wrapper, a watchable outcome, and creator seeding measured by conversion.

  1. Choose the output first. If someone cannot understand the result without narration, it is a weak short-form product.
  2. Choose one distribution loop. Short-form, build-in-public, or search. Don’t launch into all three.
  3. Charge for the first useful result. Subscription for repeat use; one-time payment for a tangible deliverable.
  4. Measure proof, not views. Cal AI’s system priced creator deals around revenue per 1,000 views.

Reality check

  • Revenue does not equal profit; only Photo AI’s reported 87% margin gives a clear case-level margin.
  • “Zero ad spend” refers to early founder-reported growth. Cal AI and Chatbase added paid channels later.
  • RizzGPT’s exact pricing is not published, and its portfolio revenue moved across reports.
  • HeadshotPro’s revenue is a single-source estimate, not independently verified.
  • Arcads’ 2026 revenue is approximate and comes from secondary podcast and event notes.
  • Photo AI’s latest verified figures here are late 2025; later founder posts suggest lower monthly figures as annual plans and churn normalized.
