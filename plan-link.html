No signup, no server

Share your money plan as a link.

Write the plan once, get a link, send it to whoever you are planning with — a partner, a friend, a group chat. The plan lives inside the link itself. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is saved anywhere but your own browser.

How it works

Your answers are encoded into the part of the address after the #. The browser shows it, the other person opens it, and the same plan appears in their form. There is no account on either end and no database in the middle. If the link gets cut off in a text message, the person can still read it in the preview.
