Privacy

What this site does with your information.

The short version: this site exists to hand you free tools and show the work. It is not in the business of selling your information, and it does not sell personal information. Period.

Email

Some pages offer an optional email signup for the free sheet and The Come Up HQ emails. That form runs through EmailOctopus. It collects only the email address you enter. We use it to send The Come Up HQ emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Your address is never sold. You do not have to sign up to use the tools.

Page views

Some pages, not including this Privacy page, the homepage, About, Contact, or Terms, send a simple page-view ping to a first-party worker when the page loads. The script does not set a cookie or store an id in your browser. The ping includes the page path, the origin of the site you came from, and any campaign tags on the link you used. Those are stored as counts, not as a profile of you.

Fonts

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). This Privacy page, the homepage, About, Contact, and Terms use fonts already on your device and do not load Google Fonts.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site, including BG Collections picks, may earn us a commission at no cost to you. The stores on the other end of those links run their own checkout and have their own privacy policies.

External sites

When you follow a link off this site, that site's rules apply, not these.
