Straight answers

What should count as extra take-home?

Use the difference that actually reaches you after deductions, or the net amount from a dependable recurring shift. Don’t count a one-time bonus or uncertain overtime as permanent income; you can still use a one-time amount for a one-time goal.

Should I save or pay debt first?

There isn’t one answer for every household. A starter cash buffer can keep a small emergency from going onto a card, while high-interest debt costs money every month. Keep required bills and minimum payments current, then choose a split you can sustain.

How reliable is the timeline?

It assumes the same added take-home and savings allocation arrive every payday. Missed shifts, deductions, emergencies, and changing costs can move the date. Treat it as a planning estimate, not a guarantee.

Does this store my numbers?

No. The calculator runs in this page and does not ask for an account or personal identifiers. Copy or share only what you’re comfortable sending.
