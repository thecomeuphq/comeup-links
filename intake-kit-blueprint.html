Local Client Intake & Instant Follow-Up Blueprint

Goal: Capture, qualify, and engage local service leads in under 5 minutes to eliminate lost sales.

Step 1: The High-Intent Intake Form (The 4 Questions)

Replace generic "Contact Us" text boxes with these 4 high-friction qualifiers on your site or booking form:

  1. Service Requested: (Dropdown: Select main service)
  2. Project Scope / Details: (Short text: Describe the job or issue)
  3. Target Timeline:
    • Urgent (24–48 hours)
    • This Week
    • Flexible / Planning Phase
  4. Estimated Budget Range:
    • Under $250
    • $250 – $750
    • $750 – $2,000
    • $2,000+
Why this works: It weed out non-serious queries instantly and gives you the exact context needed to price the job before calling back.

Step 2: Instant SMS & Email Response Protocol

When a lead submits the form, send this automated text message within 60 seconds.

Automated SMS Script (Send from business phone/automation tool):

"Hey [First Name], got your request for [Service Requested]! I'm currently working on a job site, but I reviewed your details and will call you today at [Time Block, e.g., 4:30 PM]. Quick question so I can prep your exact estimate—what's the street address or zip code for the project?"

Automated Email Backup (Triggered simultaneously):

Subject: Received: Your request for [Service Requested]

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out. We received your request regarding [Service Requested] for the [Target Timeline] timeframe.

Our team is reviewing your project details right now. Expect a direct call or text from our direct line ([Your Phone Number]) around [Time Block] to discuss the next steps and provide an accurate quote.

If you have photos or extra details to add, simply reply directly to this email with attachments.

Talk soon,
[Your Business Name]
[Your Phone Number]

Step 3: Qualification & Callback Decision Matrix

Lead Signal Action Priority
Urgent + Budget > $750 Instant direct call within 15 minutes. Priority 1 (Hot)
This Week + Budget > $250 Call back during scheduled daily outbound window (e.g., 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM). Priority 2 (Warm)
Under $250 / Out of Scope Send automated polite decline/referral template to keep schedule clear. Low Priority

Step 4: Out-of-Scope / Low-Budget Politeness Template

Save this as a quick snippet for leads that don't meet your minimum job size:

"Hi [First Name], thanks for reaching out! Based on the details provided, your project falls below our standard minimum project size of [Your Minimum Amount, e.g., $500]. We want to make sure you're taken care of, so we recommend reaching out to [Partner / Alternative Specialist Name] at [Phone/Link]. Thanks again for considering us!"

Full System Execution Checklist

  • ✓ Host index.html and app.js on your domain or landing page builder.

  • ✓ Set up the SMS auto-responder trigger on incoming webhook payloads.
  • ✓ Import the 3-part email sequence into your email marketing platform.
