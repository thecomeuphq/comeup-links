Dear — ,

If you're reading this someday, it means I finally sat down and did the thing I kept putting off. Not because I didn't have stories — Lord knows I have stories — but because I didn't know where to start, and nobody ever asked me the right questions.

So somebody asked. One question at a time, the way you'd ask me across a kitchen table. What was your mama's kitchen like? What did your first paycheck buy? Who taught you to be strong, and what did it cost them?

By the third page I wasn't writing anymore. I was remembering out loud — and somebody was kind enough to keep it.

— with all my love, always
